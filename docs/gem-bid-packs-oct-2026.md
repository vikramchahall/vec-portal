### Experience documents — the critical gap

Most completed VEC orders (SUEZ 2018, Triveni 2016, Geo Miller 2016, SN Enviro 2015) are **outside** the experience windows in these bids. The bids will be won or lost on these certificates:

| Get from | What to ask for | Used in |
|---|---|---|

| **Hindustan Zinc** (Debari RO-DM) | Work order + performance certificate with executed value to date (Apr 2025 →) | NRL, BPCL, IOCL Digboi |
| **EIL** (BS-VI manpower, May 2018 – Apr 2020, ₹23.93 L) | Completion certificate (you likely have it) | IOCL Digboi |

> IOCL tenders accept sub-contract experience only with: (a) VEC's work order from Aquatech with rates/scope, (b) completion certificate with executed value, and usually (c) proof from the end client (HMEL) or TDS certificates / Form 26AS showing payments received.
