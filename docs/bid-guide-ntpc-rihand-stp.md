## 4. Step-by-step on GeM

1. **Log in** (close GeM in Edge / other laptops first — one session only).
2. **Bids → List of Bids** → search `GEM/2026/B/8052049` → open the bid.
3. **Download every attachment** (ITB, NIT, Technical Specification, SCC, GCC, Forms, Price schedule) and read the Technical Specification (scope) fully.
4. Click **Participate / Bid Now** → accept the GeM terms and the bid's ATC.
5. **Declarations screen**
   - MSE → **Yes** → enter Udyam number → upload Udyam certificate (EMD exemption)
   - Make in India → Class-I local supplier → local content **100%**
   - GTE question "Do you certify full compliance on Qualifying Requirements?" → **Yes** (only if your certificates truly meet it)
6. **Technical upload slots**
   - *Experience Criteria* → Attachment-3A-1 + Aquatech WO + countersigned certificate (+ HZL as backup)
   - *Bidder Turnover* → Attachment-3A-2 + CA turnover certificate + 3 years' balance sheets
   - *Certificate (Requested in ATC)* → Bid Form + Attachments 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 13
   - *Additional Doc 1 (Requested in ATC)* → PAN, GST, PF, ESI, labour licence, Udyam
7. **Price** → enter the **total lump-sum amount** for 24 months; if the price schedule asks for basic + GST, keep 18% GST and make sure the total matches.
8. **Review** every page → **Submit** → **eSign** (Aadhaar OTP of the proprietor / authorised signatory).
9. **Download the bid submission receipt** and keep it.
10. From 10 Oct, check **Bids → Participated** every day — NTPC gives only **2 days** to reply to any clarification.
