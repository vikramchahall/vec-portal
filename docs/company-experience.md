## 2. What the company does (service lines)

1. **O&M of Water, Effluent & Treatment Plants** — full operation and maintenance of Water Block, ETP and RO-DM plants: plant operation, greasing and lubrication, chemical preparation and dosing, equipment rectification, civil-unit cleaning, logbook and equipment history-card maintenance.
2. **Contract Manpower Supply** — unskilled, semi-skilled, skilled and highly-skilled manpower for refineries, smelters and process plants, with full statutory compliance.
3. **Mechanical Erection, Testing & Commissioning** — erection of mechanical equipment and allied works for STPs, as per GA drawings, through to trial run and commissioning.
4. **Pipeline Laying & Civil Works** — PSC pipeline laying, excavation, backfilling with layer-wise compaction, valve chambers, thrust blocks, barricading.
5. **Industrial Painting & Protective Coating** — piping, supports and equipment painting, underground pipe wrapping and coating, canopies and sheds for chemical areas.
6. **Site Support Services** — raw-water channel cleaning and patrolling, housekeeping, guest-house and mess management, site transportation and sludge disposal.
